Bulk Email

Send a personalized email to a large list of recipients — from your registrations, a pasted list, or an uploaded spreadsheet.

Before sending 1000+ emails: a plain Gmail account can send about 500 emails per day; Google Workspace accounts allow up to 2000/day. Sending beyond your account's limit will cause the remaining emails to fail partway through. Emails are sent one at a time with a short pause between each (roughly 2-3 per second), so 1000 recipients takes about 6-7 minutes.
